About The Resident Evil Movie Quiz

Think you know everything about the Resident Evil movies? Put your knowledge to the test with this 25-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Alice, Umbrella Corporation, the T-virus, characters, facilities, artificial intelligence, creatures and major events from the original live-action Resident Evil film series.

Each correct answer gives you 2 points, giving you a maximum possible score of 50.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Resident Evil movie quiz work?

You will face 25 questions about the Resident Evil movie series. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 50 points. Every correct answer is worth 2 points.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the characters, events, creatures, locations and details from the Resident Evil movies.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.

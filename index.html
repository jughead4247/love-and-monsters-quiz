About the Love and Monsters Quiz

Think you know everything about Love and Monsters? Put your knowledge to the test with this 30-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Joel, Aimee, Boy, Clyde, Minnow, monsters, colonies, locations, weapons and memorable details from the movie.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, giving you a maximum possible score of 30.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Love and Monsters quiz work?

You will face 30 questions about Love and Monsters. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 30 points. Every correct answer is worth 1 point.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the movie, including its characters, monsters, locations and memorable events.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
